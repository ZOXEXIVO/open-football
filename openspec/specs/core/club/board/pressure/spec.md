# core/club/board/pressure Specification

## Purpose
The pressure capability accumulates discrete supporter, media, dressing-room, financial and regulatory events into slow-moving gauges that colour board confidence rather than dictating it, scaled by how sensitive the ownership archetype is to outside opinion.

## Requirements

### Requirement: Supporter/media pressure is a slow gauge scaled by ownership sensitivity
Board pressure SHALL accumulate from discrete supporter-facing events (derby results, relegation form, star departures/arrivals) into slow-moving 0-100 gauges, and the effect of those events on board confidence SHALL scale with the owner's sensitivity to supporter opinion.

#### Scenario: Reckless rich owner vs. member-owned club face the same derby loss
- **WHEN** two clubs with different ownership types suffer the same derby loss
- **THEN** the supporter-pressure delta is identical in raw terms but its effect on board confidence differs according to each owner's supporter sensitivity
