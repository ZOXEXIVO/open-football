# core/club/staff/goalkeeping Specification

## Purpose
The goalkeeping department capability plans the succession of the club's whole
keeper pool — first team, reserves and academy — as a single, ongoing pipeline.

## Requirements

### Requirement: Goalkeeping succession is planned ahead of need
The club's goalkeeping department SHALL track a declared pecking order across the whole keeper pool (first team, reserves, academy) and run an ageing/succession clock on the current number one so a replacement is being developed before the incumbent's decline, rather than re-ranking keepers from scratch each week.

#### Scenario: Ageing first-choice goalkeeper approaches decline
- **WHEN** the department's succession clock flags the incumbent keeper nearing the age at which keepers typically decline
- **THEN** the department surfaces a succession recommendation naming the next keeper in line well before the incumbent's performance actually drops

### Requirement: Pathway starts are chosen by the step up as well as the stakes
The goalkeeping department SHALL nominate a pathway keeper only for a fixture whose stakes are low and whose standard
of football is not far above the standard the department perceives him to be assured at. When no such fixture is
coming, the department SHALL seek his minutes elsewhere through a loan, rather than nominate him for a fixture at the
first team's full standard.

#### Scenario: A congested league fixture is not a debut for an unassured keeper
- **WHEN** a club plays a league fixture at its first team's full standard, with a continental match in the same week,
  and its pathway keeper is perceived as assured only at reserve standard
- **THEN** the department does not nominate him for that fixture

#### Scenario: An early cup tie against lower opposition is
- **WHEN** the same club plays an early-round domestic cup tie against a side from a lower division, whose standard
  of football is close to the pathway keeper's perceived assurance
- **THEN** the department may nominate him for that tie

### Requirement: Loans are recommended to build assurance
When a young keeper is blocked at his club and perceived as unassured at the first team's standard, the department
SHALL recommend a loan to a club whose standard of football lies between his perceived assurance and the parent
club's standard, in preference to a club far below his assurance or one at or above the parent club's standard.

#### Scenario: A loan to the level between
- **WHEN** a blocked 20-year-old keeper is perceived as assured at reserve standard and his parent club plays at
  top-flight standard
- **THEN** the department's loan recommendation targets a club whose standard lies between the two

### Requirement: A shaken keeper is protected
When the department perceives a keeper as shaken (low perceived confidence after a run of errors), it SHALL advise
withholding him from high-stakes fixtures while the keeper room has another fit senior keeper. It SHALL lift that
advice once its perception of him recovers. The manager SHALL act on the advice according to the department's
authority, as with its other advice.

#### Scenario: Taken out of the firing line
- **WHEN** a number one makes errors leading to goals in three consecutive matches and his club's deputy is fit
- **THEN** the department advises starting the deputy in the next high-stakes fixture

#### Scenario: No protection without cover
- **WHEN** the same keeper is shaken but no other fit senior keeper is available
- **THEN** the department does not advise withholding him
